      Scoring Guide

      How every KPI score is calculated, rated, and submitted. Numbers on this page follow the live system settings.

      On this page
      1

      Step 1. Enter your realization

      Type the actual result you achieved this month, in the unit of the KPI.

      Example: 35 days
      2

      Step 2. It becomes a tier score

      Your result is compared with the target and the multiplier, then lands on one of five tiers.

      1.0 0.8 0.6 0.4 0.2
      3

      Step 3. Weights build the role index

      Role index = Σ (Weight × Tier Score) × 100, so heavier KPIs move the index more.

      Example: 20% × 0.8 = 16 points

      Tier scoring by label

      T is the target and M is the multiplier, the tolerance step set for each KPI in the Master KPI Library.

      Rules that shape your score

      • balance Weights total 100% per roleEach role holds at most 10 KPIs, and their weights add up to exactly 100%.
      • date_range Quarterly KPIsThey are filled only in March, June, September, and December. In other months they show as Off-cycle and score 1.0 automatically once you submit the role, so your index can still reach 100.
      • science January is a trial monthJanuary scores are shown for practice but never count toward your annual average.
      • monitoring Annual scoreThe average of the counted months that have a score. Months without a submission are left out, not counted as zero.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why is a KPI locked?expand_more

      A KPI is locked when it is quarterly and this is not a quarter month, when the period is closed or past its cut-off, or when Admin / HR deactivated it. Locked KPIs keep their history. An off-cycle quarterly KPI needs no value: it counts as tier 1.0 times its weight once you submit the role that month.

      Why does my index look low when my filled KPIs are good?expand_more

      Every KPI weight counts toward the role index, so a monthly KPI left empty adds 0 points. Fill all monthly KPIs to see your real index.

      Why is there no score yet?expand_more

      Scores appear after you submit. The annual average also waits for at least one counted month, and January never counts.

      Can I change a submission?expand_more

      Yes, twice per role each month while the period is open. Each new submission that changes a value or the evidence file uses one change. After that, ask HR / Admin to allow another change, or to reopen a closed period.

      I forgot my password.expand_more

      Contact HR / Admin. They can email you a new one-time login code, and you will set a new password when you sign in.

      How do I reach Admin quickly?expand_more

      Use the green WhatsApp button at the bottom right of My KPIs or this guide. Pick a topic, add a detail if you like, and WhatsApp opens with your name, NIK, and the page you were on already in the message.
